Find out what has already been established on a topic before working on it.

Call `research_search` with the topic. If nothing matches, say that nothing is recorded and stop.
Do not fill the gap with what you assume the answer to be.

Read the promising ones with `research_read`. Then report:

- What the research establishes, in a few lines.
- What it leaves open, quoted from the document rather than filled in by you.
- How old it is. A document carries dated rounds; if the newest is months old and the subject moves
  (pricing, APIs, infrastructure), say that the answer may no longer hold rather than presenting it
  as current.

Where rounds disagree, the newer one wins, but say that it changed and when. The older rounds are
kept so that a reversal stays visible.

Do not merge your own assumptions into the summary. If the question asked is not the question the
research answered, say which part is covered and which part is not.
